Learnora privacy notice

Learnora processes the information needed to provide a private study workspace. This can include account information, the study content a student chooses to add, and service data needed to keep the application secure and working. Study materials, notes, tasks, schedules, and other account content are used to deliver the features the student asks for, such as organizing work, generating study aids, and saving progress.

How information is used

We use account and workspace information to authenticate users, show their saved study data, maintain the service, respond to support requests, and protect against misuse. Learnora does not sell a student’s study content or notes to third parties. AI-generated output is intended for educational support; students should review it and avoid submitting confidential or unnecessary personal information in prompts or uploads.

Your choices

Students can manage information in their workspace and can contact support@learnora.app with questions about their account or data. We may update this notice as the product changes, and the current version will be published at this address. The terms of service provide additional information about acceptable use, user content, and the limits of the service.
