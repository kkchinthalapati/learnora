About Learnora

Learnora is an AI-assisted study workspace for students who want a calmer, more connected way to plan and complete their academic work. Instead of separating schedules, deadlines, notes, flashcards, timers, and revision materials into several tools, Learnora brings the essential parts of a study routine together in one web application.

What the product is for

Students can organize subjects and tasks, keep learning materials and notes, run focused study sessions, build weekly plans, practise with quizzes and flashcards, and prepare with mock exams. The built-in study assistant is there to support active learning: it can help turn a topic into revision material, explain concepts, and suggest a practical next step. Learnora is an educational aid, so students should always check important answers against their course materials and their instructors’ guidance.

How Learnora is built

Learnora runs as a web application and uses an authenticated account to keep each student’s workspace private. Public pages explain the product and its policies, while private study data stays inside the signed-in experience. For public technical details, see the Learnora developer resources and agent guide.
