Why it looks like this

One primary action per scenario. The current Today stacks a hero, Due today, Next exam, notebooks, a resume card and a footer, with three buttons in the hero. The redesign keeps the forecast engine and brings it down to one sentence and one button.

Hierarchy

  1. The next thing (headline + one card, one button)
  2. Up to three secondary items, as rows, no cards
  3. Context: mastery ladder and study days, on the right

Study days, not streaks

A weekly goal replaces the daily streak. Nothing resets and nothing is lost. Most students here study because of an exam, not for fun, and a trade analysis of streak design notes that for that kind of user "a heavily gamified streak can feel patronising" and can add anxiety.

"Recall" ochre

The new second accent only marks memory work: due cards, topics that are fading, quick recall. Teal stays for actions. The rule is that a colour stands for one kind of thing.

Tutor drawer asks before it answers

In a high-school field experiment, students who practised with GPT-4 did worse once the AI was taken away. A tutor that gave teacher-designed hints instead of answers reduced that harm (Bastani et al., PNAS, June 2025). So the drawer asks for a guess first. "Just explain it" is always there, because the student decides.

States covered

No exam and no material → onboarding card (see 04). Everything done → "You're clear for today" plus a single optional stretch. Loading → the skeleton keeps the headline and card shape (see 05).
