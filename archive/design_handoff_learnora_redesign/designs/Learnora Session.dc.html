Response anatomy

Tutor turns are in the serif reading face with a teal rule. Student turns are compact dark bubbles. Each explanation has a one-line claim as its heading, at most three sentences of prose, then a structured part (steps, table or trap), and ends with a check. Longer depth sits behind "Go deeper".

Evidence behind the modes

Practice mixes in other topics on purpose. In a 54-class trial, interleaved practice lifted scores on a later unannounced test from 38% to 61% (Rohrer et al., 2020). Every teacher in that study also reported that interleaved assignments took longer, so the UI says "harder on purpose" to set expectations.

IA change

Solver, Explain it simply, Oral practice, Exam traps and the chat drawer were five separate tools. Now they are modes of one session with shared context, plan and autosave. The "Study" nav item opens the mode picker. Oral practice is Socratic with voice on, and Exam traps is a Practice preset.
