# Landing page benchmark (2026-10-23)

Written before the landing rebuild. The pages below were opened in a desktop
browser on 2026-10-23. Mobile layouts of competitors were not checked.

| Site | What works | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| StudyFetch | Warm paper background, serif headline with one italic word, one dark CTA. Features sit behind tabs, so the page stays short. A small playable matching game shows the product. | The proof is mostly numbers (users, "grade improvement" %, ratings) and a 4-minute video. The product itself is shown late. |
| Quizlet | Asks a question ("how do you want to study?") and answers it with a row of product-mode cards. Sign-up is one obvious button. | A generic indigo palette. The cards are illustrations, not the product working. |
| Duolingo | Clearest conversion path seen: one primary button and an "I already have an account" link. Personality comes from one colour and one character, used consistently. | Little information about what you actually do until you scroll. |
| Khan Academy | Picks the audience straight away (student, parent, teacher). | Busy navigation, and the cookie banner covers half the hero. Proof is a claim ("boosts scores"), not a demo. |
| Examigo | Strong, confident type and colour. It has a personality you remember. | Full-bleed pastel blocks, and scroll-reveal leaves whole sections looking empty while they fade in. |

## What Learnora takes from this

- **One primary action** ("Start free") with a quiet sign-in route, as Duolingo does.
- **Use tabs for depth**, as StudyFetch does, so the page doesn't become a feature wall.
- **Show the product, not numbers about it.** We have no verified user counts or
  outcome stats, so we don't use any. The proof is a demo you can play with.
- **Make the personality ours.** Learnora's identity is the exercise book: ruled
  lines, a red margin, red-pen corrections and an ochre tick (`webapp/DESIGN.md`
  §0.8). It also matches the product's real differentiator, the mistakes ledger
  and check-back loop. None of the five sites does this.
- **No scroll-gated content.** Everything is visible without animation. Motion only
  plays in response to what the visitor does in a demo.
