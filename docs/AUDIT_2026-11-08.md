## 1. Executive Summary

The second comprehensive audit conducted on 2026-11-08 confirmed that Learnora's core pedagogical loops and AI capabilities are operational on live production. Pull Request #168 successfully resolved script-src Content Security Policy violations on `/app/`, eliminating Zod JIT eval probes and establishing strict script-src and style-src controls with zero console errors. AI Marking and Guided Sessions performed reliably across 10 student user journeys: typed and handwritten student answers were awarded rubric marks with verbatim quotes, the vision model fallback handled capacity constraints gracefully, Past-You multi-turn debate enforced belief caps against prompt injection, Check-Back generated adaptive retests with remediation links, and Exam Detective cleanly deconstructed exam traps.

In file processing and social features, in-browser OpenXML decompression (`officeText.ts`) reliably parses DOCX documents into structured Markdown using native `DecompressionStream` and `DOMParser` without server-side dependencies or mammoth. Audio lecture recording cleanly captures microphone streams at 16 kbps Opus with real-time timers and a 10 MB ceiling. Friends and Study Room Realtime presence channels sync participants, cheers, chat, and a 9-preset Web Audio ambient sound synthesizer without external audio assets. Offline mode reliably mounts status banners and mutation queues.

However, eight new defects (**F-17 through F-24**) were identified and verified on live production:
1. **Broken math rendering on legacy cards** (P2, F-17): Flashcards created prior to commit `ab1c81b` (such as the Ohms Law deck) store double-escaped backslashes (`\\\\dfrac`, `\\\\Omega`) in Supabase; KaTeX parses `\\\\` as linebreaks and renders raw text `dfrac` without fraction bars or Greek symbols.
2. **Permissive CSP leak on bare `/app` route** (P2, F-18): `vercel.json` scopes strict CSP to `/app/(.*)`. Navigating directly to `https://learnora-app.vercel.app/app` (no trailing slash) falls through to the root rule, serving legacy permissive headers with `'unsafe-eval'` and `'unsafe-inline'`.
3. **Standalone marker UX copy mismatch** (P3, F-19): When vision is unavailable on `/mark`, the banner advises: "You can still type your answer or paste text", but `/mark` only contains an image upload input (`#mark-photo`). Typed answers are only supported in lesson steps.
4. **Double-escaped LaTeX in dynamic retests** (P3, F-20): Edge-generated retest questions occasionally escape math strings (`\\text{ kg}`), requiring resilient client-side JSON decoding.
5. **Past-You single-turn belief drop cap** (P3, F-21): Even a flawless Turn 1 student explanation caps belief drop at `MAX_DROP_PER_TURN = 45`, forcing a second turn (pedagogical guardrail against single-turn bypass).
6. **Tell-Apart drill abrupt redirect** (P3, F-22): Completing the 6-statement contrast drill redirects immediately to `/` (Today) without a completion toast or summary modal.
7. **Offline lazy route preload uncaught error** (P3, F-23): Navigating to an unvisited code-split route while offline causes Vite's `preload-helper` to fail on CSS/JS chunk preload (`ERR_INTERNET_DISCONNECTED`), bubbling an uncaught error out of `Suspense` instead of showing an offline notice.
8. **Zero-friend leaderboard hides user's own focus metrics** (P3, F-24): On `/app/friends`, having zero accepted friends collapses the entire leaderboard into `EmptyState` ("No friends yet"), hiding the student's own weekly focus minutes and streak.
