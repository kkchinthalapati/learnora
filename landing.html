What makes it different

Getting it wrong is the useful part.

Most study apps give you a score and move on. Learnora writes down which idea you mixed up, and every tool works from that list until the mix-up is gone.

  1. Day 1

    You get one wrong

    In a quiz, a flashcard review or a tutor session. Learnora files the idea behind the wrong answer, not just the lost mark.

    "thinks water flows to the dilute side"

  2. Day 1

    It's re-taught

    A short explanation aimed at that exact mix-up, so you're not rereading the whole chapter.

  3. Every day after

    Everything reads the list

    The tutor checks it before it answers you. New quizzes include those ideas, reviews put those cards first, and your weekly plan makes time for them.

  4. 2+ days later

    Check-back

    One question you haven't seen before, with no hints. Get it right and the mix-up counts as fixed. Get it wrong and it reopens for another re-teach.

Straight answers

Is the AI always right?

No. It's a study aid and it can be wrong. Check anything important against your course materials and your teacher's guidance. The quizzes and the mistakes list are there so you're testing yourself, not just taking its word for it.

When does Learnora use AI?

When you ask it to: writing notes, making cards or a quiz, the tutor, plans. The first time you use an AI feature, Learnora asks whether that's OK. Tasks, exams, card reviews and the timer work without it.

What happens to my notes and files?

They stay in your private account. Learnora doesn't sell your data and doesn't use it to train AI models. The privacy notice lists every AI provider involved and how long things are kept.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Learnora runs in the browser on phones, tablets and laptops, and your account is the same on all of them. There's nothing to install.

Who is it for?

Students with exams to prepare for, at school, college or university, in any subject you can write notes about.
