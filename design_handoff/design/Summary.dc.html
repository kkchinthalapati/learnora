Learnora revamp · Summary and implementation notes
Learnora revamp·The two-minute version

Open it, know what to do, do it, leave feeling better.

Learnora already does something rare. It finds the wrong idea behind a wrong answer, fixes it, and checks days later that the fix stuck. But students couldn’t see that happening. In production, 33 wrong ideas were logged and none was ever marked fixed. Practice was three or four taps away and often meant waiting 25 seconds. This revamp makes the good part obvious and the rest quiet.

1. One next step, everywhere

Today shows one sentence and one button, with a time cost ("Practise osmosis · 8 min"). The same step appears on Practise, Plan and Progress, because one engine decides it. The first question appears straight away from built-in exam-board questions.

2. Mistakes become trophies

A wrong answer gets a short, kind explanation and a date: "We’ll check this on Thursday." On Thursday, that check is the next step. It's one new question, about a minute. Get it right and you see a calm "Fixed" screen, and it joins your list of things you’ve fixed.

3. Honest, never scary

Readiness is a range ("likely a 5 or 6"), it says what it’s based on, and it ends with what to do. "Not tried yet" is never shown as weak. Boards we don’t fully support yet (CBSE, ICSE, A-level) say so when you pick them.

4. No guilt, no ambushes

After a break: "Welcome back. Nothing’s overdue." The plan has already moved, and there's a 5-minute restart. You can see AI limits before you start, a limit never stops you mid-question, and the free plan is complete.

Accessibility checks to add to CI

  • Contrast: loop every preset × mode through the same pair list the design system checks live (120 pairs). Fail the build if any pair fails.
  • Answer options use aria-disabled after Check, never disabled, so they stay focusable. Their result is part of the accessible name.
  • Every role="img" chart has an aria-label sentence and a "Show as a table" alternative.
  • The tutor drawer is not modal (F6 switches between panes). Sheets trap focus and return it to whatever opened them.
  • Reduced motion: --dur-* collapses to 80 ms fades, and the AI-working pulse stops.
