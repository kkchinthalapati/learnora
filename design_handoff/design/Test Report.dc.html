04 · What changed, and why

Baseline → Round 1, then Round 1 → Round 2

The redesign (→ R1)

These came straight from the top-10 baseline issues. They're what took the mean from {{ base.mean }} to {{ r1.mean }}.

  1. A due check-back becomes Today’s next step. The repair ends with a date, and there's a "Fixed" moment. #1 · J3 −1.3
  2. Bank questions first, with generated ones joining behind. The first question appears in 0 s. #2, #3 · J2 −1.7
  3. One next-step engine, one card shape everywhere (1c). #4
  4. Welcome back: automatic re-plan with undo, no counts, a 5-minute restart. #5 · J6 −1.2
  5. The cost is shown before starting. Limits are only checked at session start. Free options come first. #6 · J8 −1.2
  6. Plain words throughout ("mix-up", "Keep it fresh", "tutor goes"). #7
  7. Readiness as a range, plus the basis, plus a best move. Evidence-based rungs. #8 · J5 −1.6
  8. One week view with tap-your-free-slots. #9 · J9 −1.3
  9. First run: honest board labels, a 2-minute check, a proper exam name. #10 · J1 −0.9
