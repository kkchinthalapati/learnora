02 · The team's hypotheses, checked against the code

Six confirmed, two partly, plus two new findings

The September redesign already fixed a lot: five-item nav, old tool URLs redirect into one session, flat buttons, honest "untested" logic. The problems that remain are mostly about what gets shown first, not missing features.

PartlyToo many destinations; five tools diagnose wrong beliefs

The nav is already five items and Debugger, Feynman, Sparring, Viva and Exam Detective all redirect into /study/:id. But Study still shows five mode cards plus two variant links, Library has five tabs, Plan has four children, and 34 view folders remain. The overload moved from the nav into the screens.

ConfirmedSeveral "what next" engines contradict each other

At least six deciders. Today's hero comes from the trajectory forecast; Study's "Based on your work" card comes from the mistake ranking; Plan blocks come from autoSchedule. Deck topics and quiz-only topics get two different forecasts. They can name different topics on the same day.

ConfirmedThe mistake loop rarely completes

The likely cause is visibility. A due retest is a secondary row called "Check: …" under the hero, and only appears on the day it's due. The repair screen never tells you when it's coming back. There's no reminder and no moment when something is marked "fixed". The section disappears entirely if the loop migration isn't applied.

ConfirmedVery few quizzes are taken

With no notes, Today says "Add material to get a next step", even though there are 228 AQA bank questions. With a flashcard topic, the primary button starts a 25-minute focus timer and opens /timer. Practice is AI-first, and the bank is only a fallback.

ConfirmedSlow AI with nothing to do meanwhile

While it waits, Pending shows a streaming cursor, a Stop button and a caption. The error card is good: it's calm, says what was kept and offers flashcards instead. But it only appears after failure, never during the wait.

ConfirmedOnboarding promises boards that aren't there

Onboarding offers CBSE, and CBSE/ICSE Class 9, but the catalogue has no CBSE or ICSE syllabus. New: A-Level is offered too, and there's no A-level syllabus either. That affects Sam.

ConfirmedMastery labels over-claim

One correct answer gives "Recalled". Five in a row gives "Applied", whatever the question type. The first-run win screen shows the ladder after a single check.

PartlyThe welcome screen sends people into menus

Only on "Run setup again" (?replay=1) and /setup/profile. The real first run is FirstRunView: topic → mini lesson → quick win → two questions. It's value-first, which is right. Its problems are the AI wait, honest board labels, jargon on the win screen, and an exam named after the topic. The replay's recap still points into menus, including the retired Dashboard ▸ Customize.

NewThe accessibility floor is real. Noor's problems are concentrated, not everywhere

Focus moves to the heading on each step, the error card uses role="alert", and CI tests contrast and touch targets. Her friction is in charts, the week grid, and answer buttons that become disabled and drop out of the focus order.

05 · Top 10 across the panel

Ranked by severity × how many people it hits

Score = severity weight (blocker 3, major 2, minor 1) × personas affected. The design phase starts at the top of this list. That's why the rollout order is Today, then the question flow, then the mistake loop.

06 · Principles

Ten rules every screen has to pass

These are the acceptance criteria. Each screen in the final package will list the ones it serves. The examples are real copy, in the voice we'll use.

P1One obvious next step

At most one primary action per screen. Today is one sentence, one button and a time cost.

Do · “Practise osmosis · 8 min”
Don’t · Three equal “Start” buttons stacked down the page
P2Every activity has an end

You know how long something takes before you start, and you can stop at any point without losing anything.

Do · “Save and stop. You did 4 of 6, that counts.”
Don’t · A 25-minute timer as the default answer to “what now?”
P3No guilt

No streaks to break, no overdue piles, no “behind”. A missed week is something we quietly plan around.

Do · “Welcome back. Here’s a 5-minute restart.”
Don’t · “84 cards due · 3 h behind plan”
P4Honest, not flattering, not scary

Every prediction comes with a range, the reason behind it and what to do next. Untested is neutral. Thin data is said out loud.

Do · “Likely a 5 or 6. Based on 12 questions, so it’s rough for now.”
Don’t · “62% ready” · red “Weak” on a topic you’ve never tried
P5Mistakes are useful

Wrong answers get feedback that's warm, specific and short. The ledger is a trophy case of fixed mix-ups, not a list of failures.

Do · “Common mix-up: heavier things fall faster. We’ll check it again on Thursday.”
Don’t · A red cross, a score and a paragraph
P6Calm density

Lots of space and one accent. Colour only where it means something. Red only for real errors.

Do · One card, one colour of emphasis
Don’t · Badges, notification dots, counts added for decoration
P7Tools appear when they’re useful

Beginners see the core loop: Today, practise, fix, keep it fresh. Advanced tools show up when they're relevant, or when someone asks.

Do · “Full mock exam” shows up four weeks before the exam
Don’t · Notebooks, rooms and the forecast all on day one
P8Waiting is productive

While the AI works: honest progress, a rough time, and something useful to do. If it fails, there's a calm fallback that always works.

Do · “Writing 4 new questions, about 20 seconds. Try this one first.”
Don’t · A spinner on an empty screen
P9Readable for everyone

Short sentences in words a 13-year-old knows. Read-aloud and a reading mode. Every chart starts with a sentence saying what it shows.

Do · “Ask me questions” · a speaker button on each question
Don’t · “Socratic mode” · a chart with no words
P10Respect the wallet and the data

You can see your limits before you reach them, never in the middle of a task. The free tier is complete. You agree to AI once, in plain words.

Do · “Uses 1 of today’s 5 tutor goes”, shown before you start
Don’t · An upgrade prompt halfway through a question

07 · Information architecture

Keep five tabs. Change what they lead with.

I tested the starting hypothesis and mostly kept it. Two changes came out of the walkthrough. Mix-ups don't get a tab. They live on Progress and come to you on Today when a check-back is due, because a destination you have to remember is the reason the loop fails. Practise stays a tab and isn't folded into Today, because Maya and Jordan need a place that always starts something, even when Today's suggestion doesn't fit.

One next-step engine

NEW · backend

Consolidates the six current deciders. Today, the top of Practise, today's block on Plan and "what to do about it" on Progress all read from it. Every step is sized to the time you picked. The first rule that matches wins:

  1. 0Back after 7+ days → welcome back, 5-minute restart5 min
  2. 1A check-back is due → prove a mix-up is fixed1–2 min
  3. 2Exam in 3 days or fewer → mixed check on the topics worth the most marks10 min
  4. 3Unfinished session from the last 3 days → pick up where you left offleft
  5. 4A new mix-up from the last session → fix it5–8 min
  6. 5Highest marks × fading or untested topic → practise, bank questions first5–10 min
  7. 6Cards are due → keep it fresh~10 min
  8. 7Nothing else → “You’re clear”, plus an optional stretch—

With no exam and no data, rule 5 still works. It picks a starter set of bank questions from the chosen subject. "Add material" is never the only step.

Practise: four feelings, not ten tools

The student picks a feeling and Learnora picks the mode. The five old diagnostic tools become variants inside a session, so they're no longer destinations.

“I don’t get it yet”Explain mode: step by step, a check after each step.absorbs Solver
“Test me”Practice mode: bank first, generated questions join when they're ready, quick confidence tap. Timed traps and the full mock appear when the exam is close.absorbs quiz runner, Exam Detective, Pre-mortem, mock exam
“I keep getting this wrong”Fix: Socratic questions starting from a logged mix-up, with hints. Voice is optional.absorbs Debugger, Sparring, Viva
“Keep it fresh”Recall: due cards, mixed, works offline.absorbs /review/:deckId
“Let me explain it back”Teach mode. Only shown once a topic has a passed check. Ends in the debrief.absorbs Feynman studio + debrief

09 · Decided at checkpoint 1

What we agreed

Recorded here so every later screen can point back to it. Next: the design system.

CBSE and ICSE stay

Both are labelled "partly ready" at the moment you pick them. ICSE is added to the first-run chips. Bank questions are off and AI questions are marked as general.

Mastery rungs renamed and tied to evidence

Started → Remembered → Remembered later → Used in a new question → Explained. Multiple choice alone can't go past "Remembered later". This needs a change to lib/mastery.

One next-step engine

The designs assume it gets built. Today, Practise, Plan and Progress all read the same step.

First run = FirstRunView

J1 has been redone against it. The wizard is setup replay only, and moves into Settings ▸ Study.

Today: two directions

A "one card" against B "time first". Built after the design system.

IA

Practise is the tab label and the /study URL stays. No mix-ups tab. A labelled "Ask" pill. Settings in six sections.
