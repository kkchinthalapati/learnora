★ Today · 1c, the chosen direction

One card with a time chip, in every situation

The next-step engine's rules decide what goes on the card: back after a break, check-back due, exam close, or a normal day. The shape never changes, so the student always knows where to look.

★ Practise · one chooser

“How are you feeling about it?”

Ten tools became four feelings. The top card is the same next step Today shows, so opening the Practise tab and tapping Start is always 2 taps.

★ Question flow · focus mode

Start instantly, stop any time, get it wrong safely

Sessions hide the nav. The top bar holds the only exit (save and stop), progress counted in questions, read-aloud and reading mode. The question is in sans, and there's one action at the bottom.

Session end · ★ Mistake loop

Found → re-taught → checked back → fixed

Each step of the loop is visible and has a date. The check-back comes to the student on Today (see 1c) and takes one minute. Fixed things become a collection, not a list of failures.
